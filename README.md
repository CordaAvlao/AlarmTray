🛎️ AlarmTray (BeepScheduler) V2.5

AlarmTray is a modern, lightweight alarm and beep scheduler for Windows (V2.5). It runs quietly in the background and stays accessible from the system tray next to the clock.

This tool is designed to be simple, fast, and unobtrusive.

✨ **What's New in Version 2.5**
- **Next Alarm Tooltip**: Hover over the tray icon to see the next scheduled alarm and day at a glance.
- **Row-Level Activation**: Enable or disable specific alarm lines in the grid without deleting them (via the "Actif" column).
- **Time Validation**: Strict validation ensures your alarm times are always in the correct format (`HH:mm`).
- **Refined Shutdown Options**: When the shutdown alert appears, choose to proceed (OK), postpone until tomorrow (Reporter), or disable the feature entirely.
- **Visual Warnings**: Automatic Windows shutdown with clear 5-minute and 1-minute popups.
- **Light/Dark Themes**: Modern interface that respects your preference.
- **Integrated Sounds**: High-quality built-in sounds (Casio, Siren, Alarm Clock, etc.).
- **Export/Import Settings**: Easily backup or share your configuration as JSON.
- **About Info**: Integrated credits (Author: CordaAvlao) and GitHub link.
        Beep Scheduler

        Right Click: Settings

🖥️ How It Works

    Launch AlarmTray.exe
    A bell icon appears in the system tray
    Right-click → Settings
    Configure your alarm times and sound source
    Left-click → Stop alarm when it rings
    The application runs silently in the background and saves your settings automatically

🛎️ AlarmTray (BeepScheduler)

AlarmTray est un petit utilitaire Windows léger qui permet de programmer facilement des alarmes quotidiennes, avec un bip système ou un fichier MP3, directement depuis la zone de notification (system tray).

Il fonctionne en tâche de fond, ne gêne pas, et reste toujours accessible via l’icône de cloche près de l’horloge.
✨ Fonctionnalités

🖥️ Fonctionnement

    Lancez AlarmTray.exe
    Une icône de cloche apparaît dans la barre système
    Clic droit → Paramètres
    Configurez vos alarmes et votre source sonore
    Clic gauche → Arrêter l’alarme lorsqu’elle sonne
    L’application reste discrète en arrière-plan et sauvegarde automatiquement les réglages
